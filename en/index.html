What the app does

Add a note to a number you don't want to save to Contacts. When that person calls again, the incoming call screen shows who it is and what they wanted last time. Everything stays on your phone and in your iCloud.

The note doesn't show on calls

  • Turn the app on in Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification.
  • The number must not be saved in Contacts – iPhone shows the contact name instead.
  • After adding a note, open the app once so the list is loaded into iPhone.
  • If calls arrive as a small banner at the top, the text is shortened. Switch Settings → Apps → Phone → Incoming Calls → Full Screen.

How to create a group

A group works for a company, a family or friends. All members see the shared numbers and notes, including on calls. It's free. Each member needs an iPhone with this app and iCloud turned on.

  1. Open the app and tap ⋯ at the top left.
  2. Choose Group.
  3. Tap Create group.
  4. Tap Invite or remove people.
  5. Choose who gets the invitation and how to send it, for example by message or email.

How to join a group

  1. Install the app from the App Store and open it once.
  2. Open the invitation you received and tap the link in it. If iPhone asks, confirm opening it.
  3. The group's numbers download into the app.
  4. Turn on caller identification in Settings → Apps → Phone → Call Blocking & Identification, otherwise notes won't show on calls.

An invitation for a specific person works for the email or phone number of their Apple ID. If it won't open, ask the person inviting you to invite you at that address or number. You can be in only one group at a time.

How to add numbers to a group

  • New number: turn on To group when adding the note.
  • A number you already have: open it and turn on In group.
  • All numbers at once: ⋯ → Group → Add all my numbers.

Members see the name, rating, tags and note text. Photos, recordings and reminders stay on your phone only. Numbers you don't add to the group stay private.

Using and managing a group

  • When someone adds a note, it appears for the others in the app and on calls after a short while. If it isn't there yet, open the app.
  • Who can change what (from version 1.3): any member can add notes. Only the person who added a number and the group admin can delete it or change its name, rating and tags. A note can be edited or deleted by its author and the admin.
  • Inviting or removing members is up to the person who created the group: ⋯ → Group → Invite or remove people.
  • Leaving a group: ⋯ → Group → Leave group. The group's numbers disappear from your phone.
  • Deleting a group: the person who created it taps ⋯ → Group → Delete group. The group disappears for members, and the creator keeps the numbers as private.

New iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple ID and install the app – numbers and notes download from iCloud. Then turn on caller identification in Settings again.
